Privacy Policy for Clipbrary

Last updated: September 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Clipbrary ("the App", "we", "us") handles information when you use our mobile application. Clipbrary is a video-link organizer that lets you save links to videos from platforms such as Instagram, YouTube, TikTok, and X (Twitter) using your device's native "Share" option, and organize them into categories.

We built Clipbrary to work entirely on your device. There is no user account, no sign-in, and no backend server operated by us. Please read below for full details on what stays on your device and what limited information may be processed by third-party services such as advertising.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information stored locally on your device

When you share a video link to Clipbrary from another app, or set up your profile in Settings, we save the following details directly in your device's local storage:

  • The video's URL / link
  • The source platform (e.g., Instagram, YouTube, TikTok, X)
  • A thumbnail image associated with the video
  • The title, category, and card color/icon you assign
  • The date and time the link was saved
  • Any reminder notification you choose to set for that video
  • A profile picture and display name, if you choose to set them in Settings

The profile picture is selected from your device's photo library (or camera, if you choose to take one) and, like everything else in this section, is saved and displayed only in local storage on your device — it is never uploaded to us or to any server.

Important: Clipbrary does not download, host, or store the actual video file — only the link and a thumbnail preview, so you can find and reopen the video later in its original app. This information is stored only in local storage on your device. We do not have a server, and we cannot see, access, collect, or transmit this information — it never leaves your device except as described in Section 2 below.

1.2 Information we do NOT collect

  • We do not require or ask you to create an account or log in
  • We do not collect your name, email address, or phone number
  • We do not collect precise location data
  • We do not access your contacts, photos, or files beyond what you explicitly share into the app
  • We do not operate any backend server or database — nothing you save is uploaded anywhere by us

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Clipbrary uses (or, after initial release, will use) Google AdMob to display advertisements within the App. AdMob is a third-party advertising service provided by Google. When ads are shown, Google's AdMob SDK may automatically collect certain information to serve and measure ads, including:

  • Advertising ID (a resettable device identifier)
  • Device information (model, operating system, language, screen size)
  • IP address and approximate (coarse) location derived from it
  • App usage and interaction data (e.g., ad impressions and clicks)

This information is collected and processed by Google, not by us directly, and is governed by Google's Privacy Policy and AdMob's data collection practices.

2.1 Ad personalization and your choices

Where required by law (for example, for users in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland), we obtain your consent before showing personalized ads using Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent tool. You can change your ad preferences at any time:

  • On Android: Settings → Google → Ads → "Opt out of Ads Personalization" / "Delete advertising ID"
  • Via Google's own controls: adssettings.google.com

3. Notifications

Clipbrary lets you optionally turn on a reminder notification for an individual saved video. These notifications are scheduled and delivered locally by your device — we do not use a push-notification server, and no data about your notifications is sent to us or to any third party. On Android 13 and above, the App will ask for notification permission the first time you try to use this feature; you can allow or deny it, and change it later in your device's App Settings.

4. Permissions We Request

PermissionWhy we need it
Internet access To load thumbnail previews and to display ads via Google AdMob
Notifications (Android 13+) To show the optional reminder notification you set for a saved video
Photos / Camera To let you choose or take a profile picture in Settings — the image stays on your device

5. How Your Information Is Used

  • To display your saved videos, organized by category, inside the App
  • To show the thumbnail so you can recognize a saved video at a glance
  • To deliver a reminder notification if you set one for a video
  • To display advertising and measure ad performance, via Google AdMob

We do not use your information for any purpose beyond making the App work and, where applicable, showing ads.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade any information. Because Clipbrary has no backend, the only data that ever leaves your device is the limited technical/advertising information described in Section 2, which is processed by Google AdMob according to Google's own privacy practices.

7. Data Retention & Deletion

All video details you save exist only in local storage on your device. You can delete any saved video at any time from within the App. Uninstalling Clipbrary, or clearing the App's storage from your device's Settings, permanently deletes all data the App has stored — we have no copy of it anywhere, because we never received one.

8. Children's Privacy

Clipbrary is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with information, please contact us so we can address it.

9. Third-Party Platforms

Clipbrary lets you save links to videos hosted on third-party platforms (Instagram, YouTube, TikTok, X/Twitter, etc.). Opening a saved link takes you to that platform's own app or website, which has its own privacy policy and practices that we do not control. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of those platforms separately.

10. Your Rights

Because we do not collect or store personal data on any server, most data-access and deletion requests are already fully in your control on-device (see Section 7). For information collected by Google AdMob, you can exercise your rights (access, deletion, opting out of personalized ads, "Do Not Sell/Share" for California residents, etc.) directly through Google's Data & Privacy controls.

11. Security

Since your saved data stays on your device and is not transmitted to any server we operate, it is protected by your device's own security (screen lock, OS-level app sandboxing, etc.). We recommend keeping your device and operating system up to date.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example if we add new features or third-party services. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
