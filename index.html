Privacy Policy for Clipbrary

Last updated: September 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Clipbrary ("the App", "we", "us") handles information when you use our mobile application. Clipbrary is a video-link organizer that lets you save links to videos from platforms such as Instagram, YouTube, TikTok, and X (Twitter) using your device's native "Share" option, and organize them into categories.

We built Clipbrary to work entirely on your device. There is no user account, no sign-in, and no backend server operated by us. Your saved video links, thumbnails, categories, reminders, display name, and profile picture are stored locally on your device.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information stored locally on your device

When you share a video link to Clipbrary from another app, or set up your profile in Settings, we save the following details directly in your device's local storage:

  • The video's URL or link
  • The source platform, such as Instagram, YouTube, TikTok, or X
  • A thumbnail image associated with the video
  • The title, category, and card color or icon you assign
  • The date and time the link was saved
  • Any reminder notification you choose to set
  • A profile picture and display name, if you choose to add them

Your thumbnail images and profile picture are stored locally on your device. They are not uploaded to Clipbrary servers because Clipbrary does not operate a backend server.

Important: Clipbrary does not download, host, or store the actual video file. It saves the video link and a thumbnail preview so you can recognize the saved video and reopen it later in the original app. This information is stored locally on your device.

1.2 Information we do not collect

  • We do not require you to create an account or log in.
  • We do not collect your name, email address, or phone number on our servers.
  • We do not collect precise location data.
  • We do not access your contacts.
  • We do not upload your saved video links, thumbnails, categories, reminders, display name, or profile picture to our servers.
  • We do not operate a backend server or database for storing your saved Clipbrary content.
  • The current release does not include advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting SDKs.

2. Local Storage and Backup

Clipbrary stores your saved video links, categories, reminders, display name, and profile picture locally on your device. We do not operate a server that receives or stores this information.

If your device automatically backs up application data through Android or another backup service, that backup may be controlled by your device or operating-system provider. Clipbrary does not independently control those backup services.

3. Notifications

Clipbrary lets you optionally turn on a reminder notification for an individual saved video. These notifications are scheduled and delivered locally by your device. We do not use a push-notification server, and Clipbrary does not send your notification details to our servers.

On Android 13 and above, the App may ask for notification permission when you first try to use this feature. You can allow or deny the permission and change it later in your device's App Settings.

4. Permissions We Request

Permission Why we need it
Internet access To open video links, load thumbnail previews, and access the original video platforms.
Notifications To show optional reminder notifications for saved videos.
Photos / Camera To let you choose or take a profile picture in Settings. The selected image is stored locally on your device.

5. How Your Information Is Used

  • To display your saved videos inside the App.
  • To organize saved videos by category.
  • To display locally stored thumbnail previews.
  • To display your selected profile picture and display name.
  • To schedule reminder notifications that you choose.
  • To open saved links in their original platforms.

We do not use your locally stored information for advertising, analytics, or marketing.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your information. Clipbrary does not upload or share your saved video links, thumbnails, categories, reminders, display name, or profile picture with us or with third parties.

Clipbrary does not include advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting services in the current release.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

All video details, thumbnails, reminders, display name, and profile picture stored by Clipbrary remain on your device. You can delete saved videos from inside the App.

Clearing the App's storage through your device settings or uninstalling Clipbrary deletes the data stored by the App on that device. We do not have a server-side copy of this information.

8. Children's Privacy

Clipbrary is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us.

9. Third-Party Platforms

Clipbrary lets you save links to videos hosted on third-party platforms, including Instagram, YouTube, TikTok, and X/Twitter. Opening a saved link takes you to that platform's own app or website, which has its own privacy policy and practices that we do not control. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of those platforms separately.

10. Your Rights and Choices

Because Clipbrary stores your content locally on your device, you can manage, edit, or delete your saved information directly inside the App. You can also remove the App's stored data through your device settings or uninstall the App.

You can control notification and photo or camera permissions through your device settings.

11. Security

Since your saved data stays on your device and is not transmitted to any server we operate, it is protected by your device's own security, including your screen lock and operating-system protections. We recommend keeping your device and operating system up to date.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example if we add new features or third-party services. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
